How to use the OopBuy spreadsheet

  1. Save the exact listing. Record the item link, color, size and quoted price. Reopen the listing when you are ready to order.
  2. Compare measurements. For shirts and jackets, compare the listed chest width and length with a garment you own, measured flat. For shoes, confirm whether a size chart gives foot length or insole length.
  3. Check the warehouse photos. If your agent offers QC photos, verify the size tag, color and condition. For a jacket, check the zipper and cuffs. For shoes, compare both soles and inspect the seams. Confirm any exchange deadline before approving shipment.
  4. Estimate the delivered cost. Add the item price, domestic delivery, agent fees and international shipping. Check whether taxes or duties apply and whether the shipping quote uses actual or volumetric weight.

Common questions

What is an OopBuy spreadsheet?

It is a browsable list for comparing product finds before using a shopping agent. This independent page displays a selection from the CAFANS catalog.

Can every item be ordered through OopBuy?

Open the CAFANS listing to see its buying options. Confirm that your chosen agent accepts the original seller link and that the exact variant is available.

Where do I find the final price?

Check the current listing price and request a shipping quote for your destination. Ask your agent which fees and taxes are included.
